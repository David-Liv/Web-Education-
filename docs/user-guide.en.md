# HOKSE Trial User Guide

Updated: 2 November 2026. HOKSE helps you plan, understand and review your own learning. It is a personal study assistant, not a school grading system.

Website: [Open HOKSE](https://web-education.onrender.com). Sign in with the account sent to you privately. You do not need to register, install software or run Docker.

**Change the whole interface:** select English or 繁體中文 on the sign-in page or at the top right. Navigation, forms, calendar controls, quiz actions, messages and administrator settings change together. Your choice is saved in this browser and survives refreshing and signing in again. Choose separately on other computers. You can also use the [English entry link](https://web-education.onrender.com/?lang=en). The guide follows the interface language, and both guide versions can still be selected and downloaded.

**Interface language and learning content language are separate.** An English interface does not translate or rewrite original questions, Chinese passages, your answers, notes or conversation history. Existing teaching material and external websites may remain in their original language. Set the learning / AI content language under University Goals → Grades & available time → Learning content language. Existing conversations retain the content language selected when they were created. Switching the interface does not submit or clear unfinished forms, change scores or alter admission scoring rules.

Share the cloud website link above. The organiser's computer does not need to stay on, and you can turn yours off when you finish.

**AI features require an enabled shared service and sufficient credits.** Saving a URL/key or fetching the model list does not activate AI. Until then, fixed practice, notes, goals and the calendar remain available; subjective answers show reference points rather than invented scores. The free website may take longer to open after being idle.

This guide uses the English interface labels, with Chinese names alongside key controls to help you switch between languages.

## 1. Your first five steps

1. Open My Learning Flow (我的學習流程) and choose Maths, Chinese or English.
2. Set your target level and daily study time, then save. University choices and complete subject grades can wait.
3. Choose three questions and try Fixed guided practice (固定隨堂練習). Choose Fixed independent quiz (固定獨立小測) to work independently.
4. Write answers, save for later or submit for feedback. Hints are available and recorded as assistance.
5. Read the feedback, preview a two-week plan, and confirm before it enters your calendar. Use Daily Review afterwards.

Saving daily time here replaces the budget for all seven days. Use Goals to set different weekday and weekend budgets. You do not need to add study time just to fill the calendar.

## 2. What each sidebar item does

- **My Learning Flow / 我的學習流程**: three-subject starting points, short explanations, fixed or AI exercises, provisional skills, corrections and next steps.
- **Study Calendar / 溫習日曆**: scheduled tasks, unscheduled checklist, two-week plans, weekly milestones and rest days.
- **Daily Review / 每日溫習**: today's, yesterday's and due material, recall cards, notes and short checks.
- **Study Overview / 學習總覽**: activities, tasks, reviewed evidence and reports. Some charts cover only their own evidence source.
- **My Subjects / 我的學科**: subject and topic directory. A listed topic is not necessarily available for teaching or assessment.
- **AI Study Room / AI學習室**: saved conversations, explanations, guided reading and writing advice.
- **Topic Quizzes / 專題測驗**: the original reviewed-bank diagnostic, practice, retest and timed quiz workflow. Published content is required.
- **Mistake Notebook / 錯題簿**: original-bank mistakes, possible causes and corrections. Coaching feedback is kept inside each coaching exercise.
- **Learning Resources / 學習資源**: official links, tools, external question metadata and bookmarks.
- **University Goals / 升學目標**: subject grades, targets, time and JUPAS programme information.
- **User Guide / 使用指南**: read, switch language and download this guide.

Personal Settings lets you export your records and sign out. Only authorised administrators or reviewers see the content/developer workspace.

## 3. Current subject coverage

Each subject has a representative starting set, not a complete course.

- **Maths**: quadratic roots, conditions, simple word-problem modelling and step feedback. AI independent quiz generation currently supports quadratic-root questions that code can verify from coefficients.
- **Chinese**: original short reading passages, evidence, inference, classical-Chinese assistance and paragraph expression. Paste your writing for advice. This is not a complete prescribed-text course or official composition grading.
- **English**: reading evidence, inference, vocabulary/grammar and paragraph organisation. Paste a passage or draft for help.

Chemistry keeps its earlier entry points. The original reviewed-bank packs cover quadratics and acids/titration; formal bank availability depends on publication.

## 4. Choosing an exercise button

- **Fixed guided practice / 固定隨堂練習**: no AI call to generate questions. Chinese/English subjective feedback and modelling-step evaluation still require a model.
- **Fixed independent quiz / 固定獨立小測**: answer fixed original questions independently. No AI question-generation call; hints and repeats are still recorded.
- **AI guided practice · quick / AI隨堂練習 · 快速**: one generation call, without the full quiz repair workflow.
- **Generate independent quiz / 生成獨立小測**: AI questions are checked before use, with up to three generation attempts if repair is needed.

Only AI independent quiz question generation uses the full check-and-repair workflow. Chat, small exercises attached to guided reading, writing advice and submitted-answer feedback use a fast, single-response approach.

If generation fails, stop, retry later or choose fixed practice. A generation failure is not a zero on your record. A language question passing format checks does not mean an expert has verified its meaning.

## 5. Understanding answers, feedback and progress

Save unfinished work and reopen it under Continue Previous Practice. After submission, read your answer, reference feedback, possible error cause and next step.

- **Code-checked reference answer**: a numeric or multiple-choice answer was compared using rules; this is not professional review of the whole question.
- **AI reference**: a one-call evaluation of subjective writing or steps. It can be wrong.
- **Unknown / not assessed**: unanswered, insufficient information or no usable evaluation. This is not zero.
- **Provisional skill state**: a working estimate for choosing what to study next, separate from whole-subject DSE levels and reviewed evidence.

Practice reference, Initial understanding and More consistent (provisional) apply only to the skills actually covered. Hints, seen answers, same-topic chat during a check and repeated question families do not endlessly add independent evidence. A final numeric answer without modelling steps leaves method ability unknown.

If feedback is wrong, choose This Feedback Has a Problem (這個回饋有問題) and explain why. The original record remains, while the disputed feedback is excluded from skill calculations. After repeated difficulty, read an example or change method. You can explicitly continue; earlier weaknesses are retained.

After a check, continue strengthening, preview maintenance and a subject switch, or inspect a higher-target scenario. HOKSE does not silently change your target.

## 6. AI chat, guided reading and writing

Paste a passage, draft or question in My Learning Flow. Choose Guided reading / explanation + quick practice (導讀／講解＋隨堂題) or Writing suggestions (寫作建議). The text is prefilled in a conversation; press Send to call the model.

You can also create conversations directly in the AI Study Room, choose a supported unit/mode, search, rename or delete conversations, and add useful learning to Daily Review.

Explanations, step-by-step help, reading and writing advice are text-based. Asking for two small questions after a reading explanation gives them in the normal reply, without starting the full quiz workflow.

Listening, speaking and Teach AI features labelled as demonstrations are not validated live assessments. The current model receives text only; attaching an image or audio does not mean it has understood it.

During an unsubmitted original-bank diagnostic or timed quiz, same-unit AI teaching is blocked. Chat assistance during a coaching check affects whether that attempt counts as independent.

## 7. Calendar, checklist and rest

Add tasks yourself or preview a 14-day study plan. It uses goals, skills, prerequisites and available time. Nothing enters the calendar until you confirm.

- Check Keep in Checklist (先放清單，稍後安排時段) to retain a task and its estimated duration without a time slot.
- Schedule/Edit (安排／調整) moves that same task into the calendar; it is not a duplicate.
- Mark tasks done, skipped or locked; unlocked tasks can be rescheduled.
- Rest This Day (這一天先休息) moves that date's unfinished, unlocked study tasks back to the checklist. Your weekly budget is unchanged.
- Restore Scheduling (恢復安排) restores that day's capacity. You decide when to place checklist tasks; there is no automatic backlog dumped into tomorrow.

Exam, locked and completed records remain. Weekly milestones update with current tasks; an empty week can remain flexible. If a preview expires after goals, time, rest or records change, generate a fresh preview.

The complete official 2028 DSE timetable is not yet verified. Demonstration dates are not official exam dates.

## 8. Daily Review and the two-week report

Daily Review combines recent and due learning. Choose the tool you need:

- **Summary and recall cards**: try to recall before revealing the answer. Turning a card is not an independent correct answer.
- **Daily checks and subject drills**: practise supported material. Coaching can open a fixed independent check.
- **Mistake retry**: revisit original-bank mistakes; for coaching, reopen feedback and choose your next step.
- **Study notes**: save, edit or delete your own notes.
- **Focus timer**: a front-end timer, not proof of effective attention.
- **Evening wrap-up**: optional notes about what is clear and what remains uncertain.
- **Teach AI**: demonstration feedback is not a real speaking grade.
- **Original review report**: the earlier recall/evidence summary.

Open the Two-week Review (雙周學習回顧) from coaching, Daily Review or Overview. It uses the latest 14 days of actual records: active days, tasks, review, answers and provisional skills. With only a few days of data, it shows that limited coverage rather than inventing a trend. Recorded activity duration is not effective focus time.

## 9. University goals and JUPAS

You may select one main programme and up to two backups, plus whole-subject grades and targets. Baseline grades should come from school estimates or broader mock exams, not a few short questions.

Programme entries show sources, years, recorded requirements and weightings. Missing formulas or grades prevent a reliable precise ranking. Verified examples may use their original year, such as 2027; they are not verified 2028 rules.

A weighted-score scenario is a calculation under stated assumptions, not an admission probability. Improvement-time estimates still require assumptions; HOKSE has not learned how long every student needs to rise a level. Conflicts with necessary entry requirements are shown.

## 10. Resources and the Maths By Topic index

Search resources, filter by subject or price, bookmark and visit the source website. Demonstration sponsor cards are not established real partnerships.

The maths index supports topic, year, source, difficulty and publisher-target filters, plus CSV downloads. It contains 585 past Paper 2 metadata rows, 23 Oxford authored-exercise rows and collection links. View original questions and explanations at their sources.

Website difficulty and publisher labels such as 2+/4+/5** are not official per-question DSE grades. Unknown stays unknown. The index is not the reviewed in-app assessment bank and does not automatically add mastery evidence.

## 11. Original Topic Quizzes and Mistake Notebook

Original Topic Quizzes use published packs for diagnostics, practice, retests or timed checks. Unanswered, graded and manually pending answers are distinct. Critical steps do not enter reviewed evidence until checked.

The original Mistake Notebook shows answers and candidate causes. Choose a better cause or uncertain. Correcting one answer is not stable mastery. This workflow remains separate from provisional coaching feedback.

Content Awaiting Review does not block the whole platform: use supported coaching exercises in My Learning Flow.

## 12. Accounts, attachments and your data

Use your own account; goals, chat, notes and calendar records stay separate. Usernames are case-insensitive and support 2–40 characters. Passwords must match exactly. There is no public self-registration or self-service password reset; contact the organiser for help.

Selected trial accounts can receive a private 3–5-character password from the organiser. Use the latest invitation. A reset invalidates the old password and sessions, so sign in again; your learning records remain. Normal invitations and first setup still require at least 10 characters. Keep administrator credentials private.

Export your records and sign out in Personal Settings. Sign out on shared computers. The older demonstration version uses browser storage and is separate from real account records.

Attachments support specified images/audio, up to 20 MiB per file and a 100 MiB shared attachment cap in the free cloud pilot. They are visible to their owner or an authorised review process. The current AI receives text only. PDF paper OCR, marked-paper import and automatic diagnostic exemptions are not implemented.

**AI logs and privacy:** administrators can view each user's model, Plan, request time, status, duration and tokens, and export AI response text for troubleshooting. The detail screen excludes response text. AI call logs retain up to 1,000 records for 30 days. Credit ledgers and orders remain separate; this does not delete chat or learning records. Avoid unnecessary personal secrets in your questions.

## 13. Administrator setup

Students do not enter API keys. Open the content/developer workspace from Personal Settings:

1. Create student accounts under Invite Accounts. Grant reviewer access only for content-review duties.
2. Under Model Service, enter the shared API Base URL, protocol and key, then fetch the model list. Discovery does not test every model.
3. Select an admin test model and test it. Tests use the actual provider API but do not deduct student credits. Enable/apply the shared connection, then manage plan defaults, allowed models and prices under **Plan settings**.
4. Existing students start on Free with no retrospective charges. Change assignments under **User plans & credits**. Plan changes do not erase this month's used/reserved credits. Changes retain the actor, time and before/after values.
5. Newly discovered models are not automatically available. Add, price and enable them, then grant plan access. Replace plan defaults before disabling a model. Students can select allowed models but cannot read the API key. A blank key retains it for the same URL; changing URLs requires a new key.
6. **AI usage logs** support user/model search, status and Hong Kong date filters, paging and refresh. The eye icon shows request summaries, tokens, errors and settlement. Response text is available only in single JSON or filtered JSONL exports; keep exports private. Reviewers cannot read admin logs or edit billing rules.
7. A text test does not verify teaching quality, images or paper OCR. Original reviewed packs still need separate reviewer accounts before publication; coaching does not wait for this. Never share the admin account or API key.

School/parent/tutoring-organisation portals, real payments, mentor matching, social features and round-the-clock psychological support are not part of this version. A simulated receipt is not proof of payment.

Website updates run automated tests, security checks and a build before being published to the same address. There is currently one live environment. These checks use AI fixtures and temporary databases; they do not validate real model teaching quality.

## 14. If something goes wrong

- Slow first visit: the free service may be starting. Wait and try again; do not register another account.
- AI unavailable: ask the administrator to configure/check the model. Use fixed practice meanwhile; unavailable subjective feedback is not zero.
- Generation failed: choose fixed questions or retry later rather than repeatedly clicking.
- Incorrect feedback: flag it or ask the chat to explain its evidence.
- Calendar has no room: shorten, move or keep the task in the checklist; you do not need to inflate your budget.
- Original-bank answer awaiting review: wait for the reviewer or continue with other coaching exercises.
- Wrong classification or source: send the page, question/source reference and a short explanation to the organiser.

Useful trial feedback says what you were trying to do, where you got stuck and what you expected. You do not have to try every feature first.
