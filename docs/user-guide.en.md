# HOKSE User Guide

v1.10 · 28 November 2026 · Find your direction and make progress at your own pace.

Website: [Open HOKSE](https://web-education.onrender.com). Sign in with your username or verified email, or create an account with a 6-digit email verification code (and class passcode when required) when self-registration is enabled. Visitors can also browse public posts in the [Student Forum](/forum) before signing in; sign in to post, reply and vote. No software installation is needed.

The sidebar is grouped by task: Study Overview, Daily Review and Study Calendar come first (the invited version also shows My Learning Flow), followed by My learning, Ask & discuss (AI Study Room and Student Forum) and Explore more (Learning Resources and University Goals). User Guide (and Plan & Billing in the invited version) sits at the bottom of the sidebar. After signing in you start on Study Overview.

On desktop, use the button at the top of the sidebar to collapse navigation to icons. Hover over an icon or focus it with the keyboard to see its name; the Daily Review count stays on the icon corner. Your choice is remembered in this browser. On mobile, a bottom shortcut bar gives one-tap access to Overview, Daily Review, AI Study, Forum and More, or you can open the full menu from the top bar.

## 1. Getting started

1. Choose your subjects and enter your current and target grades.
2. Set the time you can spend studying each day. You can choose university goals later.
3. Pick a unit in My Learning Flow and start with an explanation or a short exercise.
4. Preview your Study Calendar and add the plan when it suits you.

Use school estimates or mock exams as a starting point for whole-subject grades. Short exercises identify topic weaknesses; they do not automatically become a whole-subject DSE grade.

## 2. Subject topics and My Learning Flow

Clicking any topic in Subject Learning opens a full-page Direct Topic View immediately — with no pre-topic target-grade or available-time forms in the way (target grades and daily study time belong solely to schedule building in University Goals and Study Calendar).

- **Compulsory Mathematics — Factorisation (7-part direct guide & interactive practice)**: The top overview shows your overall independent `%` progress alongside clickable **Part 1 to Part 7** cards (common monomials, common brackets, two-part `(a)`-into-`(b)` grouping, single-variable quadratics, two-variable homogeneous quadratics, difference of two squares & sign traps, and cubic identities & Paper 2 prime-substitution checking). Scroll down to read concise 3-step methods, worked examples and exam traps, then complete each part's Guided Warm-up and 2 Independent Checks (49 original isomorphic variants in total). Algebraic equivalence and complete factorisation are checked instantly by code with **zero AI credits**; assisted or retried solves never inflate independent mastery, and “Try a fresh variant unassisted” lets you prove genuine unassisted mastery on a new variant anytime. Long explanations are split into per-part chunks for the AI Explainer: click “Ask AI to explain this part / question” to pre-fill a focused step-by-step chat draft (no credits are used until you send).
- **Other ready topics**: View your progress, core essentials, formulas and worked examples on one page, then start practice or ask the AI tutor in one click.

### DSE Scientific Calculator Simulator (Default fx-50FH II · Switchable to fx-3650P II)

When you focus a Maths or Chemistry answer input (in the Factorisation guided course, Quizzes, My Learning Flow or AI Study Room), a 1:1 dual-line LCD scientific calculator can open automatically beside your workspace (toggle “Auto-show” to remember your preference, or open anytime via the inline input icon or the top-bar calculator pill — including during multiple-choice questions and Open Focus mode).

- **Dual models & photo-aligned secondary keys**: Defaults to **`fx-50FH II`** (brushed metallic silver faceplate with coral-orange **`Prog` / `FMLA`** pills, built-in Maths formulas `01` Quadratic Equation, `02` Cosine Theorem, `03` Heron's Formula, `04/05` Normal $P(x), Q(x)$ with Physics `06–23` marked for Phase 2, plus all 40 official scientific constants `CONST 01–40` on `SHIFT + 7`; `680 bytes` program memory), switchable in one click to **`fx-3650P II`** (with numerical integration `∫dx`, numerical derivative `d/dx` on `SHIFT + ∫dx`, and `390 bytes` memory). Every amber `SHIFT`, red `ALPHA`, and blue bracketed mode legend (`SHIFT + (` → `%`/`arg`, `SHIFT + )` → `Abs`, `SHIFT + ,` → `;`/`Conjg`, `SHIFT + EXP` → `π`, `x³`/`∛`, `x⁻¹` `x!`/`LOGIC`, `+`/`−` `Pol`/`Rec`/`▸r∠θ`/`▸a+bi`) is aligned 1:1 to physical hardware and works authentically.
- **High-frequency DSE Maths modes**: Full `COMP`, `CMPLX` (rectangular & polar complex numbers), `SD` (1-variable statistics with `DT`/`S-SUM`/`S-VAR`) and `BASE-N` (`DEC/HEX/BIN/OCT` + bitwise ops); `REG` is scheduled for Phase 2.
- **Native `MODE 6 1` programming & DSE 6-in-1 Presets**: `P1–P4` start empty just like a fresh physical calculator. Write or edit programs directly on the keypad via **`MODE → 6 → 1`** with a full Casio BASIC interpreter (`?→A`, `:`, `◢`, `Lbl`/`Goto`, `⇒` conditionals and `SHIFT 3` `P-CMD`), or open the side **Presets** drawer (Quadratic Formula 01+, Cosine Rule SAS/SSS, Simultaneous 2×2, Circle & Line Intersection, Triangle Centres G/O/H, Cubic Roots) to load any preset into `P1–P4` or view exact physical keystrokes.
- **Input integration & cloud sync**: Use **Insert Formula & Result** (which automatically inserts clean numeric values into numeric-graded boxes) or **Copy Answer** (copies only the final value), and press `Alt+C` to explicitly toggle physical keyboard control without ever stealing input focus. In invited mode, your custom `P1–P4` programs, memories and model choice sync automatically to your account across devices (saved locally in demo mode); just like real HKDSE exams, using the calculator never reduces independent mastery evidence.

In My Learning Flow, Mathematics, Chinese and English also offer unit explanations, exercises and feedback. Choose a subject and a starting question count (without repeating grade or daily-time forms), then pick what you need:

- Fixed quick practice: try a method and revisit it at your pace.
- AI quick exercises: generate short supporting questions.
- Independent quizzes: answer on your own before reviewing feedback and next steps.
- Guided reading and explanations: bring an article or question into a conversation.
- Writing suggestions: improve content, structure and expression step by step.

Use hints when needed. After submitting, review each answer. If something seems wrong, select “There is a problem with this feedback”. AI feedback is study guidance; discuss uncertain points with a teacher or tutor.

### Focus on one skill

In Mathematics, use “Focus on one skill, then try something new”. Choose standard form and coefficients, factorisation, the quadratic formula, the discriminant, parameter conditions, or contextual modelling. Read the short explanation and try one to five questions, with three as the default. Review a prerequisite first when needed.

Enter numbers, fractions or expressions such as (5+sqrt(13))/2. For modelling, also enter the equation you formed. A final number alone does not establish the method. An expression that cannot be reliably checked is saved for further reference checking.

“Download practice PDF” uses the same questions and numbers. Print it, then follow its link back to enter your answers. Repeat exports use no AI credits.

Use “Question this judgement” to explain what should be checked. Each answer can be reviewed automatically once, at no credit cost to you. The result may correct or uphold the judgement, or leave it uncertain and excluded. Your original answer is retained; reviewing it does not create another attempt.

## 3. AI Study Room

Choose a teaching method when starting a conversation. David offers guided reading, gradual hints and step-by-step problem solving. Matt builds short PDF lessons around your goal, followed by practice and discussion. Matt’s first complete topic is quadratic equations in compulsory Mathematics; the selector shows which topics are available. When an approved **Partner Tutor licensed Teaching Playbook** is published for a unit, the selector displays it alongside built-in methods in a single step with highlights and a static tutor profile (clearly labelled as AI-guided using that tutor’s licensed method, not a live human reply; no ads inside chats or PDFs, and existing plan model pricing stays unchanged; if a method is archived later, past chats remain readable and your shared skill evidence stays intact). Invited Partner Tutors record and check these methods themselves in a separate Partner Tutor Studio, and each one goes live only after the platform reviews copyright, compliance and a sandbox chat. Your grades, mistakes, forum posts and built-in tutor chats are never shared with Partner Tutors.

Each conversation keeps its tutor. “Change tutor” opens a new conversation while keeping the original. Relevant learning notes can carry across. Teaching settings do not change your grade targets or calendar. In “Learning settings”, add your goal, constraints and preferences, and revisit lesson references and learning notes. Confirm notes that match your understanding to help your tutor teach what comes next.

Matt provides a downloadable PDF for a new lesson. Ordinary follow-up questions stay in the chat. Choose “Next PDF lesson”, check the draft and send it when you want to continue. After reading, choose “Discuss this lesson” and include its lesson number, version and question number, such as “Lesson 1, v1, Q2”, followed by your answer or question. Lessons provide questions first; ask for hints or a worked answer whenever you need them.

“Revise content” fills a draft for you to edit and send. Revisions create a new version; earlier versions remain available. Opening and repeat downloads use no credits. A new lesson or content revision costs one operation at your conversation model price, including automatic exercise checks. An undelivered lesson is not charged. For file layout problems, use the free rebuild option under “Lesson details” to reformat the saved content. Downloading or reading a lesson does not automatically establish formal mastery.

New exercises and worked answers in chat are checked before appearing. General explanations may appear first, with practice added to the same reply afterward. For requests that only ask for questions or solutions, wait for the complete reply. These checks help reduce errors; replies remain AI learning references.

Started work continues when you switch conversations or leave the page. Return to see saved results; use Stop to cancel explicitly. In invited mode, you can switch between your plan-allowed conversation models directly from the AI Study Room composer bar. On transient upstream errors, the backend automatically retries up to the administrator-configured count (default 3) before returning a failure with the reason; if a reply still could not be completed, Retry continues the same saved request once without an additional charge or rewriting an explanation already delivered. After another failure, send a new request.

Each conversation keeps a learning topic together. Rename it, continue asking questions, stop a reply or save a learning summary.

Replies can include tables, formulas, charts, function plots, basic geometry, concept maps and simple circuit schematics. Enlarge a diagram, download SVG/PNG, or open its supporting text or data where available. These are learning references; circuit diagrams are not simulations.

Case-by-case explanations can use relationship diagrams and comparison tables. Chat does not currently offer draggable sliders or interactive records with live calculations. To change example values, continue in the same conversation. Quick-practice answers can still contain mistakes; if an answer disagrees with its explanation, ask the AI to recalculate.

Suggested next steps appear when your tutor chooses to include them. Click one to fill the input box, edit it if you wish, then press Enter to continue in the same conversation. Nothing is sent by clicking a suggestion. If it replaces an existing draft, use “Restore your draft” to get that draft back.

When your tutor uses chat exercises, quick practice stays in the chat. Try answering below, or expand “Give me a hint” and “Show answer and explanation” when needed. These exercises support learning and do not count as independent quiz scores.

Suggestions, answer reveals, enlarging and downloads use no extra credits. Sending a new question is a new AI operation at the conversation model price shown in Plan & Billing. Automatic review has no additional student charge. No usable delivery means no charge; a delivered explanation retains its original charge. A previously refunded request is charged its original operation once if its retry succeeds. A reply may still provide useful text when a visual cannot be displayed.

Include the question, your own thinking and where you got stuck. With an image or recording, also describe the problem in text. Attachments currently support keeping and viewing material; attaching a file does not mean AI has read everything in it.

Listening and speaking features labelled “Demo” are for trying the experience, not formal listening or speaking assessment.

Choose “Answer this question” under a chat exercise, or the answer button for a PDF question, then send your answer directly. A small label identifies the question. Close it to return to ordinary discussion. You can also specify the lesson, version and question number. Select the actual question if its identity is unclear. Ordinary questions are not scored as answers. Tutors share your skill history while retaining the tutor and document version used for each attempt.

## 4. Daily Review and mistakes

Daily Review brings recent learning and due reviews together. Recall key points, try a short quiz, add notes or revisit a conversation summary.

Focus sessions offer two modes to match either in-app study or printed Past Paper drills:

- **Task focus**: Start from your next scheduled task on Study Overview or from the Focused Study tool in Daily Review. The timer stays in the top bar (and mirrors the live countdown in the browser tab title) while you move between lessons, the AI Study Room and quizzes. Immersive desk mode tucks away the sidebar, mobile bottom bar, notifications and search by default; use the expand/collapse icon beside the timer to show or hide navigation, or use the top-bar breadcrumb to return to Study Overview. Ending early offers a **5-second** Undo option, and finishing a scheduled task countdown shows a one-tap “Mark task done” button.
- **Open focus (full-screen guard with free in-site exploration)**: Select “Open focus” at the top of Today's learning checklist on Study Overview (or inside Daily Review's focus tool) at any time, choose **15, 25, 45 or 75 minutes**, and optionally add a custom label such as a Past Paper drill. Starting enters browser full screen (keeping the screen awake where supported) and pins a compact focus guard bar at the top while **letting you explore every page inside HOKSE freely** (lessons, quizzes, AI Study Room, Mistake Notebook, Student Forum and notes). Browser-level focus restrictions run alongside your session: external links are temporarily blocked, supported desktop browsers (such as Chrome and Edge in full screen) enable Keyboard Lock to guard against accidental `Esc` and tab-escape shortcuts, and switching tabs, changing windows or exiting full screen triggers a reminder while logging departure count and duration (exiting full screen also shows a one-click re-entry screen). Browsers cannot block OS-level shortcuts such as the Windows key, `Alt+Tab` or `Ctrl+Alt+Del`. Leaving before `00:00` still requires selecting “Cancel focus” and confirming. A “Scratchpad” button in the focus bar (and on the completion screen alongside your departure summary) lets you jot down tricky question numbers or concepts and carry them into the AI Study Room or Student Forum in one click.

Active timers resume across page refreshes. Both focus modes and your daily completed open-focus minutes are stored in this browser only; they never write to recorded study minutes or count towards mastery evidence.

After targeted practice, the skill appears under “For your next practice”. Activities for the same skill and learning date are merged. Start a retest with one or two new questions, or practise early. Moving to the next calendar date alone does not establish stable understanding: at least 24 hours, different question families and independent performance are considered together. Calendar changes still require a preview; items that do not fit remain unscheduled.

The Mistake Notebook keeps answers and possible error causes. “Practice answers to revisit” also shows these practice answers, feedback and sources, with a link to try new questions. Find the step that went wrong, then use a different question to check understanding. Reading a solution, using hints or repeating the same question helps practice but does not by itself establish secure mastery.

## 5. Study Calendar and checklist

The calendar suggests activities around your available time and learning direction. Preview before adding them. You can then reschedule, adjust time slots or keep activities in the checklist.

Drag a pending task on desktop, or touch and hold before moving it on a phone or tablet. Ordinary swipes still scroll. Day/week views snap to 5-minute intervals; month view changes the date only. Duration stays unchanged. Preview the destination while dragging and release to save. A “Schedule updated · Undo” reminder lasts **3 seconds** and can undo the most recent move. Click a task to edit its details.

Completed tasks, locked tasks and exams cannot be dragged. Overlaps, over-budget days, rest days and moves past midnight are rejected with a specific reason. Short tasks use a single-line title; hover or open the details to read the full text.

- Lock activities you want to keep in place.
- Move an activity back to the checklist when the day is too busy.
- Set a rest day and return when it suits you.
- Daily Review is already included in your time budget.

Check the source and status shown for exam dates. Use officially announced timetables for exam preparation.

## 6. University goals

Explore programmes by institution, qualification, study area or keyword. Sort by admission-score index or competition ratio. Choose one main goal and up to two backups, then compare their requirements and features side by side.

Details show interview arrangements, recorded places, subject weightings, entry requirements and historical scores. Upper quartile, median and lower quartile are displayed separately; unavailable data stays unavailable.

After entering grades, you can see:

- How far your current and target grades each fall below or above the reference benchmark.
- Current or target reference lines on the historical chart.
- Which subject improvements help the main goal and which backups also benefit.

Figures are approximate gaps: platform reference scenarios, not admission probabilities or guarantees. Borrowed programme data is labelled with its benchmark and sources. QS rankings only indicate a possible upward or downward bias; they do not add or subtract points. Open “View scoring method and reference basis” to see the subjects and assumptions used.

Study plans focus on the main goal. After changing it, preview a new plan before deciding whether to apply it.

## 7. Overview and reports

Study Overview opens with Today's learning checklist. Your next unfinished task comes first: “Start focus session” starts the timer and takes you to that task. Tick off the other tasks in the list (with Undo if ticked by mistake), or start a “25-min open focus” once today's tasks are done. Ask & discuss beside it continues your last AI conversation or opens the Student Forum. The greeting follows Hong Kong time. This week's recorded study mins (counted from completed lessons and practice activities, separate from the browser focus timer), more secure topics, your university direction and subject performance sit below the checklist. The Two-week Review brings together your recent records so you can adjust your next steps.

With limited records, start by looking at the days you have. Time recorded and tasks completed are not the same as learning outcomes. Notice whether you can explain ideas independently and handle new questions.

### Starbean (Interactive Study Companion)

The “Starbean” companion appears in the top bar and workspace corner, using ear movement, expressions and short status bubbles to reflect real-time AI and study progress:

- **Live AI & Focus reactions**: Pulls out a miniature laptop to lie down and type (with gently wiggling back feet) while AI is thinking, streaming, generating a quiz or grading, then flops flat beside the laptop once finished (“totally wiped out!”) with a one-click jump link if you are on another page. During Focus Mode, Starbean wears round reading glasses quietly at the edge, pops out with a playful nudge if you return from tab switching, and celebrates finished focus sessions, ticked tasks and strong quiz results.
- **3-stage idle animations, drag & stay distraction-free**: After 15 s idle, Starbean sits down to watch you work (its eyes subtly follow your mouse pointer); after 45 s it rolls playfully side to side; after 120 s it slips on a dark sleep mask for a horizontal nap and wakes as soon as you type or click. Drag Starbean anywhere, snap to left/right edges for quiet peeking, double-click to pet, or single-click for the quick menu (Start Focus, DSE Calculator, Ask AI, “Play with Starbean” triggers, plus 4 local colours and 4 accessories saved in your browser without affecting study records or credits). During timed quizzes or immersive focus it docks quietly to the edge, and “Hide for now” tucks it into the top-bar pill anytime.

## 8. Resources and Maths By Topic

Search, filter and bookmark learning websites in Resources. Maths By Topic helps you find questions by topic, year and source, then visit the original website.

Source difficulty labels and target levels help you find material; they are not official per-question DSE grades. External content, fees and terms are set by the source website.

## 9. Student Forum

Open [Student Forum](/forum) under Ask & discuss in the sidebar, from Study Overview or from the sign-in page to share study tips, ask questions or relax with fellow DSE students. Official boards include All Feeds, Lounge / Treehole, Admissions / JUPAS, 23 Category A DSE subject boards (with your selected subjects pinned first) and Applied Learning / Other Languages; user-created sub-boards are not supported. Sort by Hot, New or Top and filter by flairs.

Your first interaction assigns a separate forum nickname (defaulting to “Student XXXX”, editable to 2–20 characters) that stays completely isolated from your login username, email, real name, grades and mastery records. You can also post or reply anonymously on a per-post or per-comment basis: an anonymous OP replying anonymously in the same thread appears as “Anonymous OP”, while other anonymous students are numbered “Anonymous Student #1, #2” within that post only and reset across posts.

- **Visibility and sharing**: “Public (WhatsApp link preview)” lets signed-out visitors browse read-only and share links; “Signed-in students only” (default in Lounge / Treehole) restricts titles, content and images to signed-in accounts, showing only a sign-in prompt to visitors.
- **Formatting, compressed images and polls**: Posts and comments support Markdown and LaTeX maths formulas. Posts can attach up to **3 images** (automatically compressed in your browser to **200 KB** or less each; no video or large-file uploads) and include single-choice polls with 2 to 6 options where signed-in students can view vote shares and switch choices. Comments use collapsible nested threads (visually indented up to 3 levels, with deeper replies labelling the parent author).
- **Positive reactions and human-only boundary**: Posts and comments offer positive upvotes and empathy reactions (“Me too / Keep going / Useful”) with no downvotes or negative scores. The forum is **100% human-only**: AI never auto-replies, and browsing, posting, replying or voting uses **zero AI credits**. Choose “Ask AI Tutor privately in Study Room” on any post to pre-fill a private chat draft (charged at your conversation model rate only if you send it).
- **Asking from mistakes and practice**: From the Mistake Notebook, submitted quiz results and Maths By Topic, select “Ask / Discuss in Forum” to pre-fill a clean question reference card without exposing personal scores or unreleased answer keys; unsubmitted quizzes cannot be shared. Forum activity never counts toward personal practice evidence or mastery.

Opening the top-bar bell does not clear unread items automatically; unread indicators clear only when you open “View forum reply” or choose “Mark all as read”. You can edit or delete your own posts and delete your own comments. Inappropriate content can be reported; **3 distinct** student reports automatically hide the item pending moderation review. Posts containing severe distress signals display Hong Kong 24-hour youth crisis helpline details. Demo mode stores forum posts locally in your browser; real mode persists them in the server database and seeds only 3 official HOKSE starter posts without fabricating student accounts.

## 10. Personal settings and AI credits

Adjust subjects, grades, language and available time in Personal Settings, where you can also link or update your verified email. Once linked, you can sign in with your email address and reset a forgotten password on the sign-in page using a 6-digit verification code (valid for 10 minutes). Plan & Billing tracks available credits on a **rolling 30-day cycle** and lets you choose separate default models for quizzes, conversations and assessment; channel prefixes are hidden automatically so each model displays its clean base name and credit price per operation.

- **Plans and two-tier model access**: **Free** (HK$0 / 30 days) includes **100 credits / 30 days** (about 50 questions, with a rolling 7-day cap of **30 credits / week**) on lightweight Lite models, plus Study Overview, basic Calendar (viewing and manual tasks), Daily Review, basic Learning Resources, fixed practice, the Factorisation guided course and Student Forum. **Plus** (HK$39 / 30 days, **2,000 credits**, about 1,000 questions, Most popular) unlocks full access to all enabled AI models, advanced Calendar features (auto-planning, drag rescheduling and rest-day protection), the Maths By Topic Index, **AI-made Topic Quizzes** (silently adapted to your practice performance behind the scenes) and AI learning plans. **Pro** (HK$79 / 30 days, **5,000 credits**, about 2,500 questions) adds **48-hour boosted forum posts** (including anonymous posts without revealing identity), **real-time progress syncing** on Study Overview, **AI + Teacher-made hybrid quizzes** in My Learning Flow, and **Weak-spot reports & proactive alerts** (notifying you via Starbean's speech bubble or a non-blocking corner card when repeated struggles, high hint reliance or paused skills are detected).
- **Upgrade carryover and resets**: Upgrading immediately starts a fresh 30-day cycle and stacks your remaining unused credits onto the new plan allowance (for example, upgrading from Free with 100 credits left gives 2,100 credits; upgrading after spending all 100 gives 2,000 credits). Renewals and downgrades reset to the target plan's standard allowance at cycle end; Free allowances never roll over.
- **Anti-abuse registration safeguards**: Self-registration requires verified email OTPs, blocks common disposable email domains and cross-provider alias duplicates, and enforces a 24-hour per-IP new-account cap in open registration mode (relaxed in class-passcode mode so shared school Wi-Fi classrooms are unaffected).

AI operations reserve credits first and charge when a usable result is available. Failure or cancellation releases the reservation if nothing usable was delivered. An explanation already delivered retains its original charge. Fixed-question generation and rule-only marking use no AI credits. If credits run out, you can still keep notes, do fixed practice and view your calendar.

The current checkout is a demonstration. Choosing a payment channel does not charge real money. The page shows your current allowance and plan arrangements.

## 11. Common questions

- AI cannot reply: try again later, or start with lessons, notes and fixed exercises.
- The plan does not fit: shorten an activity, reschedule it or keep it in the checklist.
- Feedback or information seems wrong: note the programme or question and tell the organiser what needs attention.
- The site takes time to open: allow it to load before continuing.

Use your own account and sign out before leaving a shared computer. Sign-in and registration now include versioned Terms of Service & Privacy Notice consent (current version `2026-11-26-v1`). Built-in tutors (David/Matt), grades, mistakes and forum posts are never shared with external tutors, and you can toggle off de-identified Partner Tutor chat sharing anytime under Settings → Privacy Consent & Partner Tutor Chat Settings (exports always strip image attachments and assign a fresh random anonymous ID per chat). For troubleshooting, administrators can view AI usage records and export response text. Avoid unnecessary personal secrets in your questions.
