## 1. Getting started
<!-- guide: {"id":"getting-started","group":"free","plan":"Free"} -->

Sign in, configure your subjects, and get comfortable with the workspace in three quick steps.

### Step 1: Sign in or browse the Student Forum first
![HOKSE sign-in screen annotated](/guide/en/01-01-login.webp)
- **①** Switch between **Traditional Chinese** and **English** at any time.
- **②** Sign in with your username or verified email (or register / reset password via a 6-digit email code when enabled) and accept the terms.
- **③** Visitors can browse public posts in the Student Forum read-only before signing in.

### Step 2: Pick subjects, grades and daily study time
![First-time onboarding setup annotated](/guide/en/01-02-setup.webp)
- **④** Select your DSE subjects, current/target grades and daily study time (university goals can wait until later).
- **⑤** Save your setup to enter Study Overview straight away.

### Step 3: Navigate with the grouped sidebar and bilingual search
![Grouped sidebar and search annotated](/guide/en/01-03-navigation.webp)
- **⑥** The sidebar is grouped by task: workspace, My learning, Ask & discuss, and Explore more (mobile shows a bottom shortcut bar).
- **⑦** On desktop, collapse the sidebar to icons (your browser remembers this choice; the Daily Review badge stays on the icon).
- **⑧** Use the top search bar (`Ctrl K` / `⌘ K`) to search topics, chats and resources in either Chinese or English.

## 13. Personal Settings and AI Credits
<!-- guide: {"id":"settings-billing","group":"account","plan":"Free"} -->

Manage your profile, verified email, privacy preferences, rolling 30-day credit allowance and model defaults in one place.

### Step 1: Link verified email and manage privacy consent
![Personal Settings and privacy controls annotated](/guide/en/13-01-settings.webp)
- **①** Update subjects, grades, language and study time, and link a verified email for email sign-in and 10-minute 6-digit password reset codes.
- **②** Toggle **de-identified Partner Tutor chat sharing** off or on anytime (applies only to future Partner Tutor chats; strips images and personal identifiers).

### Step 2: Compare Free, Plus and Pro plans & credit rules
![Plan & Billing 3-tier comparison annotated](/guide/en/13-02-billing.webp)
- **③** All plans run on a **rolling 30-day cycle**: **Free** (HK$0 · 100 credits / 30 days, rolling 7-day cap of 30 credits, Lite models), **Plus** (HK$39 · 2,000 credits, Most popular, all models + 4 Plus features), and **Pro** (HK$79 · 5,000 credits, all Pro exclusives).
- **④** **Upgrade carryover & resets**: Upgrading immediately restarts your 30-day cycle and stacks unused remaining credits onto your new allowance; renewals and downgrades reset cleanly to the target allowance at cycle end (Free credits never roll over).

### Credit billing & account safeguards

- **Reserve-then-settle**: Credits are reserved before an AI call and charged only when usable output is delivered; full failures or cancellations release the reservation (a delivered explanation keeps its original single charge).
- **Zero-credit actions**: Fixed quizzes, local Factorisation polynomial checks, hints/answers reveals, chart zoom/downloads, repeat PDF downloads, one free re-check per answer, and all Student Forum actions use **0 AI credits**. You can still read lessons, do fixed practice and view your calendar if credits reach 0.
- **Simulated checkout**: Current checkout is a demonstration — selecting a payment channel never charges real money or collects real card numbers.
- **Anti-abuse registration**: Self-registration requires verified email OTPs, blocks disposable email domains and cross-provider alias duplicates, and limits 24-hour sign-ups per IP in open mode (relaxed for class-passcode classrooms).

## 14. Common Questions
<!-- guide: {"id":"faq","group":"account","plan":"Free"} -->

Quick answers to everyday hiccups alongside key platform boundaries and privacy commitments.

- **AI cannot reply or first load is slow**: Wait a moment and retry (transient upstream errors retry automatically and never charge on full failure), or work on direct lessons, notes and fixed practice first.
- **Schedule feels too tight**: Shorten tasks, drag/reschedule, set a rest day, or keep optional items in your checklist.
- **Questioning a grade or programme detail**: Use “Question this judgement” for a free automatic re-check, or note the question/programme and share it with the organiser.
- **Unread notification badge stays on**: Opening the bell never auto-clears unread items; open a reply or click **Mark all as read**.

### Key boundaries & privacy notice

- **Study references only**: Short exercises, AI feedback and JUPAS “approx. gap” numbers are learning references — never official DSE grades or admission guarantees. Maths By Topic difficulty labels come from external sources/publishers rather than official per-question grades. Features marked “Demo” (such as listening/speaking demos) are not formal assessments.
- **Browser-local focus timers**: Task focus and Open focus timers are saved locally in your browser and never count as recorded study minutes or mastery evidence. Browsers cannot block OS shortcuts such as Windows, `Alt+Tab` or `Ctrl+Alt+Del`.
- **Terms & privacy (`2026-11-26-v1`)**: Use your own account and sign out on shared computers. Built-in tutors (David/Matt), grades, mistakes and forum posts are never shared with external tutors. Administrators may inspect AI usage logs strictly for troubleshooting — avoid submitting unnecessary personal secrets.
